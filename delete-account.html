Delete Account & Data

Because Audio Productivity is an offline-first application, you do not have a traditional user account on our servers, and we do not store your media files or personal information in the cloud.

How to Delete Your Data

If you wish to completely remove all data associated with the app, simply follow these steps on your device:

  1. Delete Processed Files: If you saved any output files to your device's photo gallery or file manager, you can delete them manually just like any other media file.
  2. Uninstall the App: Delete the Audio Productivity app from your device. This will automatically erase the app's local storage, including your project history and saved preferences.

Managing Subscriptions

If you subscribed to a premium plan, deleting the app will not cancel your subscription. To cancel your subscription, please use your device's built-in subscription manager:

For iOS (Apple App Store):

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap your name at the top of the screen.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Select Audio Productivity and tap Cancel Subscription.

For Android (Google Play Store):

  1. Open the Google Play app.
  2. At the top right, tap your profile icon.
  3. Tap Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  4. Select Audio Productivity and tap Cancel subscription.

Anonymous Identifiers

The third-party services we use (like Google AdMob and RevenueCat) may generate anonymous identifiers linked to your device. When you uninstall the app, the local identifiers are cleared. Furthermore, you can reset your device's Advertising ID at any time via your device's Privacy settings.
