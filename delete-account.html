Delete Account & Data

Because Audio Productivity (Audio Pro) is an offline-first application, you do not have a user account on our servers, and we do not store your media files, recordings, transcriptions, or personal information in the cloud.

How to Delete Your Local App Data

You can clear specific data inside the app or remove all app data from your device at any time:

  1. Clear Conversion History & Temporary Cache: Open the app, go to Settings > Storage & Data, and tap Clear Temporary Cache or Clear Conversion History.
  2. Delete Downloaded AI Speech Models: Open Speech to Text, view the model selector, and tap the delete icon next to any cached Whisper model to remove it from your device's STT folder.
  3. Delete Exported Files & Playlists: Delete playlists inside the Library tab, or delete exported audio and subtitle files via your device's Files app or media gallery.
  4. Uninstall the App: Deleting Audio Productivity from your device permanently erases all local databases, saved preferences, playlists, and cached files.

Managing Subscriptions

If you subscribed to an Audio Pro plan, uninstalling the app will not automatically cancel your subscription. To manage or cancel your subscription, use your device's built-in store settings:

For iOS (Apple App Store):

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap your name at the top of the screen.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Select Audio Pro (Audio Productivity) and tap Cancel Subscription.

For Android (Google Play Store):

  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. At the top right, tap your profile icon.
  3. Tap Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  4. Select Audio Pro (Audio Productivity) and tap Cancel subscription.

Resetting Anonymous Identifiers

Third-party SDKs integrated into the app (Google AdMob, RevenueCat, and Firebase) use anonymous device or app-instance identifiers for ad delivery, purchase restoration, and crash diagnostics. Uninstalling the app clears local app-instance identifiers, and you can reset or disable your device's Advertising ID at any time in your iOS or Android system Privacy settings.
