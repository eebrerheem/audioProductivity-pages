About the App

Audio Productivity is designed to give you powerful media editing tools—trimming, compressing, extracting audio, format conversion, and merging—all running locally on your device. We prioritize your privacy and performance.

Because all processing happens on your device using FFmpeg technology, your media files are never uploaded to the cloud.
