About the App

Audio Productivity (Audio Pro) is a full-featured, offline-first mobile audio studio. Designed for creator speed and complete on-device privacy, it brings multi-track editing, format conversion, acoustic effects, AI speech transcription, and a dedicated music library player directly to your phone.

Key Features

  • Multi-Track Studio Timeline: Arrange, slice, layer, crossfade, and mix single or multiple audio tracks on an interactive visual timeline.
  • Convert, Extract & Compress: Convert between MP3, WAV, AAC, M4A, FLAC, OGG, OPUS, AC3 & WMA, rip audio from video files, or shrink file sizes with smart compression presets.
  • Trim, Split, Insert & Loop: Cut with waveform precision, split recordings into multiple tracks, insert clips at exact timestamps, reverse audio, or loop segments seamlessly.
  • AI Speech-to-Text (Whisper): Transcribe audio files or live microphone speech offline into plain text, timestamped segments, SRT, and VTT subtitles across 17+ languages.
  • Studio Recorder & Text-to-Speech: Capture high-clarity voice recordings with live waveform monitoring or convert written text into natural spoken audio.
  • 10-Band EQ, Voice Effects & Cleanup: Fine-tune frequencies, shift pitch & tempo, boost volume, apply spatial echo/reverb & vocal filters, suppress background noise, and automatically truncate dead air.
  • Library Player, Playlists & Metadata Editor: Organize tracks into custom playlists with camera/gallery cover artwork, inspect audio stream specs, edit ID3 tags, and enjoy background playback.
  • Storage & Compression Analytics: Track total exports, space saved from compression, and manage local cache effortlessly.

100% On-Device & Private: All audio processing and AI transcription run locally on your device using FFmpeg and Whisper C++. Your media files and voice recordings are never uploaded to cloud servers.
