Your ultimate offline-first audio and video editing toolkit.

About the App

Audio Productivity is your comprehensive, offline-first media editing studio. Designed for creator speed and absolute privacy, it brings a full suite of professional audio and video utility tools directly to your mobile device.

Key Features

  • Convert & Extract: Convert between MP3, WAV, AAC, M4A, FLAC, OGG, OPUS, AC3 & WMA, or extract audio from videos.
  • Trim, Split & Delete: Cut precise regions, delete mid-clip segments, or split long recordings into equal parts.
  • Merge & Overlay: Combine multiple audio tracks, adjust individual track volumes, and apply echo & reverb effects.
  • Insert & Scrubber Editing: Seamlessly insert audio clips into existing tracks at exact timestamp positions.
  • Voice Effects & Equalizer: Transform vocals with pitch shift, robotic/tempo effects, and fine-tune frequencies using the 10-band Graphic EQ.
  • Noise Suppression & Silence Removal: Clean background noise and automatically detect and remove silent pauses.
  • Recording & Text-to-Speech: Record studio-quality audio directly in-app or generate spoken audio from text.
  • Metadata & Tag Editor: Edit titles, artists, albums, genres, track numbers, and embed custom cover art.

100% Offline & Private: All processing is powered locally on your device using FFmpeg. Your media files are never uploaded to cloud servers.
