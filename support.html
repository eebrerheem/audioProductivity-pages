Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Where are my processed files saved?
A: Because the app processes files locally, all output is saved directly to your device's media gallery or designated file storage folder. You can usually access them via your device's Files app.

Q: The app crashed while processing a large file. What do I do?
A: Ensure your device has enough free storage space. Video and audio processing can be resource-intensive. Closing other background apps may also improve performance.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: Subscriptions are managed directly by the App Store or Google Play Store. You can cancel auto-renewal at any time through your device's settings. See our Account & Data page for step-by-step instructions.
