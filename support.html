Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Where are my processed files and transcriptions saved?
A: Because the app processes everything locally, exported audio files, studio recordings, and Speech-to-Text subtitle/transcript files (TXT, SRT, VTT) are saved directly to your device's media storage. You can access them from the Library tab, History screen, or your device's Files app (and you can customize the default export directory in Settings).

Q: How does offline Speech-to-Text (Whisper AI) work?
A: The first time you use Speech-to-Text, the app prompts you to download a compact Whisper AI model (such as tiny.en or base) to your device. Once downloaded, all audio file and live microphone transcriptions run 100% offline on your phone—your audio is never sent to any cloud server. You can manage or delete downloaded AI models anytime inside the Speech-to-Text screen.

Q: Why does the app ask for Microphone and Camera permissions?
A: Microphone access is used only when you record audio in the Studio Recorder or transcribe live speech in Speech-to-Text. Camera access is requested only if you choose to take a new photo for custom track or playlist cover artwork.

Q: Can I use PRO features without a paid subscription?
A: Yes! You can unlock eligible PRO tools for your current editing session by watching a short rewarded video ad (up to 5 unlocks per day), or upgrade to Audio Pro (Monthly, Yearly, or Lifetime) for unlimited access to all studio tools, multi-track layers, batch queues, and zero ads.

Q: How do I restore my Audio Pro purchase on a new device?
A: Open Settings (or the Upgrade screen) and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID or Google Play account used for the original purchase.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: Subscriptions are managed directly through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. You can cancel auto-renewal at any time through your device's subscription settings. See our Delete Account & Data page for step-by-step instructions.
