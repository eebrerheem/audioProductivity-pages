Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At Audio Productivity (also known as Audio Pro, "we", "our", or "us"), your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle, process, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. 100% On-Device & Offline-First Architecture

Audio Productivity is built as an offline-first studio application. All media processing, audio editing, library playback, and AI tasks are executed entirely locally on your device:

  • Audio & Video Processing: Converting, extracting audio from video, compressing, trimming, splitting, multi-track timeline mixing, merging, inserting audio, looping, reversing, generating blank audio, volume boosting, loudness normalization, pitch & tempo shifting, 10-band graphic equalizer tuning, spatial echo, voice effects, noise suppression, silence removal, and ID3 metadata/cover art tagging are powered locally on your device using FFmpeg and native media engines.
  • On-Device AI Speech-to-Text (Whisper): Audio transcription and subtitle generation (plain text, timestamped segments, SRT, and VTT) for both imported audio files and live microphone speech run 100% offline on your device using local Whisper AI models (whisper.cpp).
  • Text-to-Speech & Studio Recording: Spoken audio synthesis and studio voice recordings are generated and saved directly to your device storage using native system APIs.
  • Local Library, Playlists & Usage Stats: Your audio library, custom playlists, playlist artwork, conversion history, and storage/compression statistics are stored strictly in local on-device databases (Hive and SharedPreferences).

We do not upload, transmit, store, or process your audio files, video files, voice recordings, transcriptions, or cover images on any remote servers.

2. Data Collection and Third-Party Services

We do not require you to register an account, and we do not collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or biometric voice data) when you use the app.

To support optional AI model downloads, app stability, advertising in the free tier, and subscription management, the app connects to the following services when an internet connection is available:

A. Offline AI Model Downloads

When you use the Speech-to-Text feature for the first time or select a new recognition model, the app downloads the requested Whisper AI model file to your device's local storage. No audio or user content is sent during or after this download; once cached on your device, transcription operates completely offline.

B. Analytics and Crash Reporting (Google Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to monitor app stability, diagnose crashes, and understand aggregate feature usage. These services may collect anonymous diagnostic data, such as device model, operating system version, app version, and crash stack traces. They do not have access to your media files or recordings.

C. Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video advertisements to non-PRO users. AdMob may collect or use device advertising identifiers (such as Apple IDFA or Google Advertising ID), IP address, coarse location, and ad interaction diagnostics to serve and measure advertisements. Daily rewarded ad unlock counts are tracked locally on your device. You can manage or reset your ad personalization preferences at any time in your device's system privacy settings, or remove all ads by upgrading to Audio Pro.

D. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (RevenueCat)

We use RevenueCat to validate and manage Audio Pro subscriptions (Monthly, Yearly) and Lifetime purchases across your devices. RevenueCat uses an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipt tokens provided by Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We never receive or store your billing details or payment card information; all financial transactions are processed securely by Apple or Google.

3. Device Permissions

Audio Productivity requests specific device permissions only when needed to perform the features you choose to use:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Requested only when you record audio in the Studio Recorder or use Live Microphone mode in Speech-to-Text. Audio is processed and saved locally on your device and is never transmitted externally.
  • Storage / Photos & Media Library Access: Requested to import audio and video files for processing, select custom cover art for tracks and playlists from your photo gallery, browse local library folders, and save exported audio, video, or subtitle files to your device.
  • Camera: Requested only if you choose to take a photo directly within the app to use as custom track or playlist artwork.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Requested to alert you when background audio jobs or batch exports complete or require attention.
  • Foreground Service & Background Audio: Used to keep long-running batch conversions active while the app is minimized and to support continuous background audio playback with lock-screen media controls in the Library player.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your media files, transcriptions, playlists, and conversion history reside locally on your device, you have full control over your data at all times:

  • You can clear your conversion history, delete temporary processing caches, or remove downloaded Whisper AI speech models directly within the app's Settings and Speech-to-Text screens.
  • You can delete exported audio files, recordings, and subtitle files through the app's Library/History views or your device's system Files app.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently removes all local app databases, preferences, and internal cache files.

For anonymous diagnostic or subscription data processed by third-party providers (Firebase, AdMob, RevenueCat), retention is governed by their respective privacy policies. Please visit our Delete Account / Data page for step-by-step instructions on resetting identifiers and managing subscriptions.

5. Children's Privacy

Audio Productivity does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any personal information has been inadvertently provided to us via a support inquiry, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new app capabilities, legal requirements, or operational changes. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
