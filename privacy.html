Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

At Audio Productivity ("we", "our", or "us"), your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Offline-First Architecture

Audio Productivity is designed as an offline-first application. All core media processing—including trimming, converting, merging, compressing, and extracting audio—is performed entirely locally on your device. We do not upload, store, or process your media files on any remote servers. Your audio and video files remain strictly on your device.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Because the app functions primarily offline, we do not require you to create an account, nor do we collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) during general use of the app.

However, we do integrate with third-party services to support the application's infrastructure and monetization:

A. Analytics and Crash Reporting (Firebase)

To improve app stability and performance, we may use tools like Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. These services collect anonymous usage data and crash logs (such as device model, OS version, and the time of the crash) to help us identify and fix bugs.

B. Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the free version of the app. AdMob may collect and use data (including device advertising identifiers like the Apple IDFA or Google Advertising ID, IP address, and location data) to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. You can manage your ad personalization preferences through your device settings.

C. In-App Purchases (RevenueCat)

We use RevenueCat to manage premium subscriptions and in-app purchases. When you make a purchase, RevenueCat generates an anonymous user identifier to track subscription status across your devices. We do not have access to your payment information (such as credit card details); all payments are processed securely by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.

3. Device Permissions

To function correctly, Audio Productivity requests the following permissions:

  • Storage/Photos/Media: To read the media files you select for editing and to save the processed output files back to your device.
  • Notifications: To alert you when background processing tasks (like long file conversions) have completed.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Since your media files are processed locally, you maintain complete control over them. You can delete your processed files directly within the app or via your device's file manager.

For data managed by our third-party partners (RevenueCat, AdMob), retention policies are governed by those respective services. If you wish to reset your anonymous advertising identifiers or manage subscription data, please refer to your device settings or the Delete Account / Data page for instructions.

5. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
